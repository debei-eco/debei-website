Why sugarcane bagasse

Waste, reimagined
into something better.

Bagasse is the natural fibre left after extracting juice from sugarcane. We give it a second life — transforming agricultural residue into durable, food-safe tableware.

01Home & industrial compostableDesigned to return safely to the earth.
02Handles hot, cold & oily foodsReliable from freezer to microwave.
03PFAS-free options availableFood-safe solutions for evolving standards.
04Made from renewable resourcesNo trees required. Less waste created.
* Composting time varies depending on conditions and local facilities.
